Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains what information Video Downloader ("we", "our", "the service") handles when you use this website. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to make the service work.

2. Information we collect

  • Video links: the URL you paste is sent to our server so we can fetch the video for you.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type, device type and request time, recorded in standard server logs for security and to prevent abuse.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to download a video.

3. How we use it

  • To process your request and deliver the video file to your device.
  • To keep the service secure, fix errors and stop misuse such as automated flooding.
  • To understand overall usage so we can improve performance.

4. Video files and storage

Videos are processed only to fulfil your request. Temporary files are deleted from our server after the download finishes or after a short period. We do not keep a library of downloaded videos.

5. Cookies

The site itself does not need cookies to work. If we add analytics or advertising services later, they may set their own cookies and we will update this page.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited technical data with our hosting provider, or with authorities when the law requires it.

7. Data retention

Server logs are kept for a limited time, usually up to 30 days, and are then deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights

You can ask us what technical data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact
