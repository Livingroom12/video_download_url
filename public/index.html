Yes. You can paste a link and download as many videos as you like without paying or creating an account.

Where is my video saved?

The file is saved as video.mp4 in your browser's default Downloads folder. On a phone, check the Files or Downloads app.

Check that the link is complete and the video is public, then try again. If it still fails, the video may have been removed.
